Manifesto

Above Calasima, at 1,100 metres, five peaks stand side by side. They are called the Cinque Frati, the five brothers. We brought their spirit to the Halles de Lyon.

Rare products, chosen one by one. People who know their story. A counter to taste them before you take them home. Corsica without the postcard. Just the very best of it.
