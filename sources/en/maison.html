Our story

Where it began

I come from Calasima, the highest village in Corsica. Above the houses stand five granite peaks: the Cinque Frati. Up there, nobody ever eats alone.

The project

Show another Corsica.

Cinque Frati was born from a simple idea: to show Lyon the Corsica it doesn't know yet. Not the beaches. The cellars, the sheepfolds and the long tables.

The method

Visit, taste, tell.

Every producer is visited. Every product is tasted. If we can't tell a product's story, we don't sell it.
