Corsica is part of who I am.

Where it began

A place where tradition and simplicity set the pace of life

My village, perched 1,100 metres up, embodies that authenticity: unspoilt nature, sincere conviviality and a rare human warmth.

With Cinque Frati, I want to recreate that Corsican spirit in Lyon: a warm space that brings together the authentic flavours of the island and a touch of modernity. The idea is to move past the clichés about Corsica and show its generous hospitality and its culinary treasures.

This project is an immersion in a rich culture, where people from Lyon and visitors to Les Halles can taste quality products, discover a way of life and feel the soul of the island without leaving the city.

An authentic journey of taste, in the heart of Lyon.

The name

Cinque Frati, five brothers

Cinque Frati is a mountain near Calasima. The name means five brothers, or five monks. Five peaks huddled together, like guests around a table.

That conviviality is what we want to bring into the shop: come alone, leave with friends, and often with a recipe.
