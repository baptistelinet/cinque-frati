The concept

Fusion

Tradition and modernity, terroir and innovation, heritage and conviviality. The raw materials of the island meet the clean lines of a contemporary market stand.

Three intentions guide the project: genuine exchange with producers, tasting on site in a setting that is sober yet refined, and a subtle immersion carried by the materials.

Layout plan

A visit in four stages

You enter by the shelves and the refrigerated display, sit down at the tasting counter, then withdraw to the premium lounge. Preparation and storage stay behind the scenes.
